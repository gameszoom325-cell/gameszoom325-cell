Hey, I’m **Ayush Singh** — a first-year B.Tech student at **SRM Institute of Science and Technology**, studying **Computer Science Engineering with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence &amp; Machine Learning**.

I enjoy turning ambitious ideas into useful software: from sleek, responsive interfaces to full-stack applications and experiments with intelligent systems. I’m especially drawn to the space where **AI, thoughtful product design, and the web** meet.
